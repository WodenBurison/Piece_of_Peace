This is a guide to other mechanics we will be using that aren't directly attached to your characters.
# Crisis Pools
This mod specifically is on pg33, but it is a mod of the Doom Pool mod on pg32. In short you use your effect die to eliminate or step down the pool of the threat until it has been dealt with completely. I adjust the story as the pool decreases.
