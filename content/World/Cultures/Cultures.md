# Cultures

Write-ups of the peoples of this world, as the party comes to know them.

- **Aldwyn** — a human kingdom; likely the party's first real contact with
  the locals.
- **Rothmark** — a second human realm, upriver/neighboring Aldwyn.
- **Skritkin** — goblin warren-clans, scattered rather than unified into
  one nation.
- **Gorthak** — orc war-bands of the highlands and steppe.
- **Vahroth** — dire wolf packs ranging the forests and tundra.

> [!info] Setting note
> Common folklore in this world treats goblins, orcs, and dire wolves as
> mindless monsters. They aren't — they're as capable of language, culture,
> and intent as anyone else. Whether the party learns that quickly or the
> hard way is a GM call.
