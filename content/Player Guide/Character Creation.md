# Prime Sets
Remember Prime Sets are dice you will hopefully include at least one from each set in your dice pool every roll. Each roll you simply pick an appropriate one to include.
## Distinctions
You will write in your own Distinctions. This is probably the hardest thing about your character and you can save for last to think about. You need 3 of them and they all start at D8. Distinctions are normally 3-4 words long, but that's a soft rule. They don't have to be something mechanical sounding, could be some kind of virtue or motto. Considering we are playing an isekai I would suggest one as a description of abilities, like "Mage of Hellfire"; the second to be more mundane, like "Eye for the Good Stuff"; the third be some kind of moral, like "Will not Stand for Injustice." If you can think of some other category, please feel free to share in the discord, I am certainly not limiting you to my suggestions.
## Affiliations
Solo, Buddy, and Team each start at D8, but you can step down one to step up another once.
## Roles
We will use the fantasy Roles on pg58: Warrior, Priest, Wizard, Rogue, Bard. To stat these out we will use an array of D10, D8, D6, D6, D4.
# Extra Sets
Unlike the Prime Sets, you will have a bit more customization with the Extras, as this is where you really differentiate your character's stuff. You will have 10 points to spend on whatever makes sense for your character. Besides the points please take the other PCs as D8 Relationships, they won't matter much in the start but they will grow in usefulness as each of you become more known in the world.
#### 1 Point:
- Gain an Ability at D6.
- Upgrade an Ability from D6 to D8.
- Unlock a SFX for an Ability.
- Gain a Talent.
#### 2 Point:
- Unlock a SFX for a Distinction.
- Upgrade an Ability from D8 to D10.
## Abilities
Each Ability in the Ability List, starting on pg 194, is actually a group of Abilities. The Descriptors and Limits are actually what defines a single Ability. For example Blast D6 with the Descriptor "Fire" would cost 1 point and Blast D6 with the Descriptor "Lighting" would be a separate point.
Limits will be very simple. All magic will have a limit of "mana desert". Magic items will have the extra limit of "gear," but will also get a free SFX.
## SFX
Making SFX is actually quite simple for some basic ones, simple choose a cost and a benefit from the lists below adding in the specifics needed. The lists are not "all" of the costs and benefits, if you can think of something roughly equal in strength, run it by me and we can workshop if need be. 
As an example lets say you have Blast D6 "Fire" as a Ability and you want your Fireball attack to be your that you specialize in for potential better damage. You can choose the cost "Spend a PP" then with the benefit "Create a D8 Asset for the Scene" to create the following SFX.
 _________
Fireball (name optional)
Spend a PP to create the Asset "Fireball!" D8.
___
Costs:
• Spend a PP
• Step down a beneficial die
• Step up a non-beneficial die
• Choose to do something risky
• Create a D8 complication
Benefits:
• Earn a PP
• Add a D6 to the dice pool
• Step up a beneficial die
• Double a beneficial die
• Step down a non-beneficial die
• Introduce a narrative detail
• Rename a complication
• Reroll a single die
• Create a D8 asset for the scene (stunt)
### Talents
Talents are simply SFX that aren't directly attached to anything. These are for those oddball quirks about your character that may not fit anywhere else.
## Relationships & Reputations
These will simply come up in play as they come up. During play you can get a Relationship at D6 or a Reputation at D4 for free.
