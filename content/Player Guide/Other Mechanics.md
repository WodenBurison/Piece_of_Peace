This is a guide to other mechanics we will be using that aren't directly attached to your characters.
# Crisis Pools
This mod specifically is on pg 33, but it is a mod of the Doom Pool mod on pg 32. In short you use your effect die to eliminate or step down the pool of the threat until it has been dealt with completely. I adjust the story as the pool decreases.
# GMCs
I have simplified GMC creation for myself. While I will, if need be, use the terms minor and major GMC, I will not make them how the book describes.
# Leveling Up
We will use the growth mod called Milestones pg 84. I will allow you to change up the Milestones every Tag scene, so don't worry too much about getting them exactly right. Upgrading Relationships and Reputations should only be done when they make sense to do so.
NOTE: You can only gain/upgrade/unlock for a trait once per tag scene. For example you would not be able to gain Energy Aura D6, then immediately upgrade it to D8 or unlock a SFX for it yet.
### Unlockables
#### 5 XP
- Gain an Ability at D6.
- Upgrade a Reputation from D4 to D6
- Upgrade an Extra Trait from D6 to D8
- Upgrade a Prime Trait from D4 to D6
- Unlock a SFX for an Ability.
- Gain a Talent.
#### 10 XP
- Unlock a SFX for a Distinction.
- Upgrade an Extra Trait from D8 to D10
- Upgrade a Prime Trait from D6 to D8
#### 15 XP
- Upgrade an Extra Trait from D10 to D12
- Upgrade a Prime Trait from D8 to D10
#### 20 XP
- Upgrade a Prime Trait from D10 to D12
